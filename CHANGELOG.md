# Changelog

Each design update gets a new version and a matching git tag (`vX.Y`).

## v1.0 — 2026-11-10
- 3D ring viewer (three.js, ray-traced diamonds, no purple tint), phone-friendly.
- Split Reset View button with front view, top view and on-hand photo.
- Details panel with gold weight (≈ 8.2 g) and estimated total weight (≈ 12.2 g).
- Served over HTTPS at https://amysring.duckdns.org (Caddy + Let's Encrypt).
- Diamond layout corrected to match the original design (center claws, gallery rail and V pavé arms, tilted heart side stones, 3.0 mm shank stones in claw prongs, side-wall pavé); same stones and 8.2 g gold. Comparison: `renders/compare_v1fix.png`.
- Front view corrected: hearts set edge-on in straight diagonal pavé arms off a wider flat gallery rail, V pavé under the rail, 3.0 mm shank stones tilted to face the front with radial claw teeth, thicker band bottom; same stones and 8.2 g gold.
- Seats and pavé corrected: every small stone sits in a smooth rounded bezel cup (no beads or sharp drilled seats), 3.0 mm stones in rounded scallop cups with coved seats, and the 1.0 mm front-face pavé staggered in a two-row zigzag over the top; same stones and 8.2 g gold. Close-up: `renders/compare_front_closeup.png`.
- Two-size zigzag on both band faces (16 × 1.3 mm outer alternating with 15 × 1.0 mm inner per face); gallery rail re-set with 32 × 1.0 mm; stone totals unchanged (62 × 1.0, 16 × 1.2, 32 × 1.3, 22 × 1.4, 14 × 3.0, 2 hearts, centre), 8.2 g gold; lighter GLB (3.2 MB).
- Wider band face (radial thickness 2.75 mm at the sides) so metal clearly separates the zigzag and 3.0 mm stones; 3.0 mm stones sunk into rounded scalloped seats with short broad rounded shared beads between them (no spike claws); 3.0 mm pitch opened to 3.4 mm; comfort/lightening channel in the inner bore keeps 8.2 g and size 5.5; same stones.
