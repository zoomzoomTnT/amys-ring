# Changelog

Each design update gets a new version and a matching git tag (`vX.Y`).

## v1.0 — 2026-11-10
- 3D ring viewer (three.js, ray-traced diamonds, no purple tint), phone-friendly.
- Split Reset View button with front view, top view and on-hand photo.
- Details panel with gold weight (≈ 8.2 g) and estimated total weight (≈ 12.2 g).
- Served over HTTPS at https://amysring.duckdns.org (Caddy + Let's Encrypt).
- Diamond layout corrected to match the original design (center claws, gallery rail and V pavé arms, tilted heart side stones, 3.0 mm shank stones in claw prongs, side-wall pavé); same stones and 8.2 g gold. Comparison: `renders/compare_v1fix.png`.
- Front view corrected: hearts set edge-on in straight diagonal pavé arms off a wider flat gallery rail, V pavé under the rail, 3.0 mm shank stones tilted to face the front with radial claw teeth, thicker band bottom; same stones and 8.2 g gold.
- Seats and pavé corrected: every small stone sits in a smooth rounded bezel cup (no beads or sharp drilled seats), 3.0 mm stones in rounded scallop cups with coved seats, and the 1.0 mm front-face pavé staggered in a two-row zigzag over the top; same stones and 8.2 g gold. Close-up: `renders/compare_front_closeup.png`.
- Two-size zigzag on both band faces (16 × 1.3 mm outer alternating with 15 × 1.0 mm inner per face); gallery rail re-set with 32 × 1.0 mm; stone totals unchanged (62 × 1.0, 16 × 1.2, 32 × 1.3, 22 × 1.4, 14 × 3.0, 2 hearts, centre), 8.2 g gold; lighter GLB (3.2 MB).
- Wider band face (radial thickness 2.75 mm at the sides) so metal clearly separates the zigzag and 3.0 mm stones; 3.0 mm stones sunk into rounded scalloped seats with short broad rounded shared beads between them (no spike claws); 3.0 mm pitch opened to 3.4 mm; comfort/lightening channel in the inner bore keeps 8.2 g and size 5.5; same stones.
- Side band re-modelled from a pixel-measured study of the reference front view: 3.0 mm stones now face sideways (table radial, centred across the band) and sit in deep tulip U-seats cut through the band wall, the metal between neighbours rising as smooth tapered prongs (old block/bead prongs removed); zigzag pavé (16 × 1.3 outer / 15 × 1.0 inner per face) in shallow flush bead seats with more polished metal between stones; softer rounded metal (filleted band and rail, bevelled boolean edges, face-weighted normals); brighter polished white-gold material and studio lighting in renders and in the 3D viewer; 3/4 render camera matched to the reference; per-view reference overlays in `renders/overlay_*.png`; same stones, 8.2 g gold, size 5.5, GLB 3.4 MB.
- 3.0 mm stone seats: the thin crescent U edges are replaced by a thick, fully rounded half-round rim (0.6 mm wire, 0.4 mm at the bottom of each U) on both band faces, running as one continuous U under every big stone and arching over the band between neighbours into blunt rounded tulip peaks; the rim sits flush with the band face; elliptical U seats; stones, positions and sideways orientation unchanged; 8.2 g, GLB 3.5 MB.
- Side-band zigzag phase-locked to the 3.0 mm stones, as measured on the reference front view: each U-shaped prong between two 3.0 mm stones is now a long thick hairpin of half-round wire with a slot down its middle, and a 1.3 mm stone sits in the rounded bottom of every U (same angle as the prong, 1.0 mm out from the bore); a 1.0 mm stone sits right under each 3.0 mm stone, in the bottom of its cup (0.65 mm out from the bore); the cup rim wraps the 3.0 mm stone, hugs the 1.0 mm stone below it and curves around the 1.3 mm stones on both sides; zigzag continues 1.0/1.3 over the top; stone totals unchanged (62 × 1.0, 32 × 1.3, 16 × 1.2, 22 × 1.4, 14 × 3.0, 2 hearts, centre); 3.0 mm stones still face sideways; 8.2 g, GLB 3.5 MB.
- U-prongs smoothed: each side's posts and 3.0 mm cup rims are now one continuous curvature-sampled centre-line swept with a single rounded profile (crowned top, full round roll into the cut, flat zero-slope blend into the band face along the cup bottoms), with radiused corners where each cup turns up into its post, a fully round tip, and a smooth elliptical slot groove down every post that ends at its 1.3 mm stone; band cut follows the same centre-line so no step shows; stones unchanged (positions, sizes, counts, sideways 3.0 mm); 8.2 g, GLB 3.5 MB (web diamonds exported with shared vertices to make room). Zoom: `renders/u_post_zoom.png`.
